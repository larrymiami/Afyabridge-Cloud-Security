## Reviewer hardening findings

The v0.10D reviewer pass found and corrected four material evidence/decision weaknesses before review-ready status:

### 1. Fabricated repository history

A version-controlled history registry could have allowed a pull request to seed invented snapshots and manufacture an apparently improving trend.

**Fix:** while the profile remains `repository-baseline`, committed metrics history must remain empty. CI still emits snapshots as evidence artifacts, while trusted persistent trend history requires a future reviewed live-stage mechanism.

### 2. Severity downgrade beneath the reporting threshold

The earlier catalogue contract validated allowed severity names but did not pin each active control to its reviewed severity. A Critical/High control could theoretically be downgraded beneath the BLOCK threshold.

**Fix:** v0.10D anchors the reviewed severity of all 18 active posture controls.

### 3. Exceptionability weakening beneath the reporting threshold

A control that currently forbids risk acceptance could theoretically have been changed to allow it, moving a finding from BLOCK to ATTENTION under a new exception.

**Fix:** v0.10D anchors reviewed exceptionability and maximum lifetimes for all 18 active controls, while permitting strictly stronger exception policies.

### 4. Standalone evidence PASS after upstream governance failure / evidence-mode overclaim

Because the evidence job intentionally runs with `if: always()`, the reporting generator could otherwise have produced a clean standalone PASS from parseable files even after the upstream governance job failed. A loosely inferred evidence mode could also have overclaimed operational state from a partial live-source change.

**Fix:** the evidence job passes the exact upstream governance result into the report; any non-success precondition forces BLOCK. The current report is explicitly fixed to `repository-baseline`, and future operational reporting requires a reviewed stage change. The report also independently reconciles summary counters against per-rule evaluator evidence.

## Evidence boundary

This validation demonstrates:

- machine-readable and human-readable repository posture reporting;
- zero-threshold fail-closed decisions;
- explicit ATTENTION semantics;
- SLA/overdue and risk-acceptance summaries;
- 7-day exception-expiry warning behavior;
- desired-state percentage and category reporting;
- portable run/ref/commit evidence identity;
- anti-fabrication history controls;
- anchored severity and exceptionability semantics;
- evaluation-summary consistency checking;
- synthetic trend direction logic; and
- integration into the protected `Security gate verdict` path.

It does **not** demonstrate:

- live CAI/SCC posture collection;
- live effective IAM or organization policy evaluation;
- real remote-state Terraform drift;
- an actual production cloud finding;
- operational remediation SLA performance;
- trusted multi-run operational trend history; or
- a production posture dashboard.

Those remain dependent on the pending live v0.7 deployment and later runtime/shift-right validation.
